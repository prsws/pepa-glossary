## Definition

The Sensory Arm's senses, muscles and voice: real-time perception, actuation and speech for the house. The only part of Pepa that acts on its own, and only through [reflexes](pepa-arm-reflex.md): direct, requested house actions under declared rules, asking a person first before anything not declared reversible ([reverse label](reverse-label.md)). Its code is `pepa-sensory-arm-house`.
