## Definition

A Pepa arm reflex is a response that an arm completes on its own, from stimulus to effect, through rules declared in advance, without consulting the Head or another arm. Others may learn of it afterward, through the arm's ledger, never before.

It applies to every arm and is the base definition: an arm may narrow it, never widen it. A reflex may consult the person and the arm's own internal tools, but not the Head or another arm. A reflex keeps no working state beyond the exchange and does not decide from history, but it always leaves a record in the [arm's ledger](arm-ledger.md).

The Pepa Sensory Arm is the worked example. Its house scope narrows the definition to direct, requested house actions under explicit, declared and guardrailed rules, and to asking a person first before anything that cannot be undone (see [reverse label](reverse-label.md)). How the human scope fits it is open.

Requests that the Sensory Arm carries out for the Head or another arm are not reflexes; see [Request from another arm](request-from-another-arm.md).

## History

- 2026-11-01: Defined by José for every arm, replacing the house-only [Reflex](reflex.md). The "no memory" part of the older wording was refined: no recall and no trends, but always a record.
