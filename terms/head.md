## Definition

The orchestrator/supervisor. Deterministic orchestration, routing, plan-execute at supervisor level. Holds no durable local state and runs no local inference. **Host: none defined.** In v0.1 the Head was hosted on `mm2010`. That was withdrawn on 2 Oct 2026; the Head host is parked until needed.

## History

- 2026-09-04: Recorded in Glossary v0.1, section 1.
- 2026-10-02: Host withdrawn. mm2010 left Head duty and is to become a media server; no host is defined. Some design documents still assume mm2010 (contradiction open).
