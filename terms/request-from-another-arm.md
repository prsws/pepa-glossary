## Definition

The Pepa Sensory Arm is Pepa's human-facing arm, so it also carries out requests from the Head and other arms: speaking a reply, delivering an alarm, acting on their behalf. These are not [reflexes](pepa-arm-reflex.md). The decision belongs to the arm that asked; the Sensory Arm answers only for carrying it out faithfully, and records it in its ledger as done at that arm's request.

Such a request is bound by the Sensory Arm's physical limits, what its body can do (no arm can ask it to see when no camera is attached), and by its own declared authority limits (for example, asking a person before an action that cannot be undone), whoever asked. Authority granted higher up does not switch off a limit the arm has declared for itself.

## History

- 2026-11-01: Decided by José as the corollary of the [Pepa arm reflex](pepa-arm-reflex.md). The physical-limits clause came first; the authority-limits clause was decided the same day.
