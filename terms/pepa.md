## Definition

A self-hosted, octopus-inspired cognitive infrastructure *blueprint* to help the elderly preserve memory, organize knowledge, and maintain agency through deterministic AI-assisted systems. Local-first, no-cloud, MIT, ~20-year horizon, documented at [ai4aging.org](http://ai4aging.org).
