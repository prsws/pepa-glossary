## Definition

The same failure weaponized: content masquerading as intent. Reaches a "closed" Pepa through the channels it is *supposed* to ingest — STT, OCR'd leaflets, caregiver messages, MCP tool results *and tool descriptions*, and Pepa's own ingested corpus of prior AI conversations.
