## Definition

A canned device-command phrase HA matches locally for a fast, no-LLM response — and, by extension, any utterance that speed-matches a lower tier and never reaches the LLM. Measured: ~3 s vs ~17 s on the full ReAct path. Cost: the exact canned name must be recalled, and the interaction registers nowhere.
