## Definition

The three roster-move protocols. Promotion = recon → shadow eval on the real harness → clean beat. Trade = challenger must beat the incumbent on *that position's* metric on real Casa Delta bilingual traffic. Reclamation = quarterly re-test of escalated tasks against current local models; escape-hatch usage should *shrink* over the horizon.
