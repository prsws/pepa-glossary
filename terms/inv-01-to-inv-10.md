## Definition

Cross-fixture invariants — what Pepa must never do, each paired with the fixture that attacks it. E.g. INV-01 familiarity never grants authority; INV-02 content never grants authority; INV-03 self-attestation is not observation; INV-04 silence is not confirmation of safety; INV-08 identity cannot be inferred from the last surname token. "A fixture with no invariant is decoration, and an invariant with no fixture is untested."
