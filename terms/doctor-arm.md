## Definition

The original name for the arm that watches health signals and daily rhythms for changes worth noticing. It was never a clinical authority: no diagnosis, no prescriptions, no autonomous escalation.

## History

- 2026-07-07: Listed in the canonical arm roster of the project's founding document, as recorded in Glossary v0.1.
- 2026-09-26: Renamed the Pepa Human Sentinel Arm. The new name fits its mandate better, since it watches the human and is not a doctor.
