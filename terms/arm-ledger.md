## Definition

What an arm observed and did, written once, owned by the arm that acted. Read by the Pepa Self Monitoring Arm (PSMA) for health and by the Pepa Auditor Arm for conformance. Its authority class is evidence. The [utterance ledger](utterance-ledger.md) is part of the house scope's arm ledger ("what was heard").

Not the [Design Ledger](design-ledger.md), which records decisions about Pepa, not events in the house.

## History

- 2026-10-02: Described as the arm's own written-once record, read by the Self Monitoring Arm and the Auditor Arm.
- 2026-11-01: Named as the ledger in the [Pepa arm reflex](pepa-arm-reflex.md) definition, and kept apart from the Design Ledger. How every reflex reaches it when Home Assistant handles a command before the arm's code sees it is open.
