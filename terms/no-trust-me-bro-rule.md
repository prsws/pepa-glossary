## Definition

Any component, decision, or behavior must be explainable, reproducible, and traceable. Refined as **"No Unexamined Trust-Me-Bro"**: uncertainty is allowed, *unacknowledged* uncertainty is not. Deterministic parts must be exact; probabilistic parts must be labeled, bounded, and observable. Litmus test — if you can't answer *what is this doing / why is it here / what breaks if I remove it* in under a minute, it violates the rule.
