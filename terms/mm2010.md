## Definition

Mac mini mid-2010 (Macmini4,1), 192.168.10.4, rebuilt on Debian 13. The Head host. Chosen because committing to it *forces* the stateless-and-redeployable property early — the real justification is not about hardware. 8 GB is a hard ceiling; below x86-64-v2, so no local numeric work. **Update 2026-10-02:** withdrawn from Head duty; to become a media server.
