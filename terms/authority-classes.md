## Definition

Five sovereign classes, each distinct on origin, mutation rules, and failure mode: **MEMORY** (what Pepa believes) · **KNOWLEDGE** (what it can cite) · **EVIDENCE** (what it observed and did — write-once, content-addressed) · **DOCTRINE** (what it must obey — human-writable only) · **MANDATE** (what it's authorized to do right now, by whom — revocable grants). Deliberately *not* classes: **LIVE STATE** (perception, not a store) and **MODEL WEIGHTS** (zero authority). Only hard ordering: Doctrine AND Mandate jointly gate actuation.
