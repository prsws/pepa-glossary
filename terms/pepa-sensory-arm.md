## Definition

Everything that turns the world into an observation passes through it, and everything Pepa does in the house or says to a person leaves through it. It has two scopes so far: the house (senses, muscles and voice) and the human (a person's physiology, collected inward only). The code named `pepa-sensory-arm` was only the house scope's front end; it is being rebuilt as `pepa-sensory-arm-house`. The human scope's code, when there is some, goes in `pepa-sensory-arm-person`. As Pepa's human-facing arm it also carries out [requests from other arms](request-from-another-arm.md), which are not reflexes.

## History

- 2026-09-04: Glossary v0.1 described it as the Home Assistant voice and chat interface, and noted the PAHA naming overlap.
- 2026-10-26: First publication of the Sensory Arm page.
- 2026-10-27: Redefined by what crosses the boundary, not by what is observed. Human telemetry counts as part of it. Reflexes defined as explicit, declared and guardrailed rules.
- 2026-11-01: Reflex redefined for every arm ([Pepa arm reflex](pepa-arm-reflex.md)); requests from other arms described as a separate thing; repository names decided: `pepa-sensory-arm-house` and `pepa-sensory-arm-person`.
