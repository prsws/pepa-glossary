## Definition

(1) No silent action. (2) No inferred intent. (3) No external escalation without internal exhaustion. (4) No commerce without explicit approval. (5) User may always ask "why now?" and get a straight answer. Plus the pre-speech check: *could an external agent with authority misinterpret this as a command?* If yes → silence or rephrase.
